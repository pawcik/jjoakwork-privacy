JJOakWork Pin Publisher – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026.

JJOakWork Pin Publisher (“the App”) is an internal tool operated by Paweł Jędruch (JJOakWork, NIP PL7952357492), used only by the shop owner to create and schedule Pins of our own handmade products, and to create and manage Pinterest ad campaigns promoting them, on our own Pinterest business accounts.

Who uses the App

The App is used only by its owner. It does not offer accounts, sign-ups or any service to third-party Pinterest users.

What data the App accesses

Through the Pinterest API, with the owner’s authorization, the App accesses only the owner’s own Pinterest account data needed to publish content: the list of boards, the owner’s own Pins, basic account information, and the owner’s own ad accounts, campaigns and their performance reports. It does not access any other user’s personal data.

How the data is used

The data is used solely to create, schedule and organize Pins of our own products on our own boards, and to create, manage and measure our own ad campaigns. All content and ad changes are reviewed by the owner before they are published.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored securely and used only by the App. We do not sell, rent or share any Pinterest data with third parties, and we do not use it to profile or target any individual user. It is used only to run our own ad campaigns through Pinterest’s own advertising tools.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the App’s access at any time in Pinterest settings. Upon revocation, stored tokens are deleted.
